Privacy notice

Draft for review, October 2026. This is a plain-language summary, not legal advice.

The short version

  • No data leaves your device, or your own home server if you set one up.
  • No accounts, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party fonts or CDNs.
  • Speech recognition runs on your device with the Vosk engine. The speech model is downloaded once from the same site that serves the app.
  • Miku's voice uses only the voices built into your device. Online voices are never used.
  • Errors are logged only on your device. You can view, copy, save or clear the log in Settings, and attach it to a GitHub issue yourself if you choose.

Optional add-ons

If you set the address of your own Home Assistant or Ollama server, what you say or type is sent to that server only. Those servers are yours; this app never sends anything anywhere else.

What is stored on your device

Your settings (voice, speed, avatar on or off), the local error log, the speech model in the browser cache, and, only if you enter it, your Home Assistant token. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes all of it.

Links that leave the app

The source code and support links open in your browser only when you choose them.

Contact
